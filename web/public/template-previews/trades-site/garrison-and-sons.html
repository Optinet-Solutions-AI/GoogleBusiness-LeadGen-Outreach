Our Story

Started by one Garrison. Run by the whole family.

We started in 2003 with a single truck, a toolbox, and a phone that rang at the dinner table. Mike took the calls his old boss couldn’t get to, fixed what needed fixing, and let the work do the talking.

Two decades later not much has changed about how we operate. The boys came up through the trade the right way — plumbing, then electrical, then learning when a repair can wait and when it can’t. We still answer our own phone, we still show up when we say we will, and we still tell you straight when something doesn’t need fixing.

Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors. That’s the only kind of advertising we’ve ever really trusted, and it’s why we treat every house like it’s on our own street.

— Mike Garrison, owner

What We Do

Plumbing

Electrical

Panel upgrades, rewiring, lighting, outlets, and safety inspections — done by licensed hands and signed off properly.

Heating & Cooling

Furnace and AC repair, installs, and seasonal tune-ups. We’ll keep an old system running honestly before we ever sell you a new one.

Renovations

Kitchens, baths, and additions managed start to finish — one crew, one point of contact, no chasing subcontractors.

Emergency Repairs
