Email Outreach Plan

How we introduce the websites we build — without getting flagged as spam
Prepared for approval  ·  June 2026  ·  Cold-email program: Website Build & Website Improvement
The problem we're solving

A link in a cold email looks like a scam

When a stranger emails a small business a link, most people won't click it — it feels like phishing — and spam filters bury it before it's even seen. Images on a first email cause the same problem. So instead of leading with a link, we earn trust step by step: a real, human-sounding note first, then a picture of what we built, and only then the link.

✕  The old way
"Here's a website I built you: [link]." Sent cold, on the first email. Reads as a scam, gets ignored or filtered to spam.
✓  Our way
A short, friendly note first. Then a screenshot that proves it's real. The link comes only once they're warm — so it actually gets clicked.
The approach

A 4-step sequence that builds trust

Each prospect gets up to four short emails, sent four days apart over about two weeks. The moment they reply, the sequence stops and our team takes over personally.

1
Day 0 — A friendly hello
Plain text only. We mention we built (or refreshed) a sample for their business and ask if they'd like to see it. Reads like a real person, lands in the inbox.
No imageNo link
2
Day 4 — Show the work
If no reply, we send a screenshot of the website we made for them. They see the quality with no risky click required. Still no link.
ScreenshotNo link yet
3
Day 8 — Send the live link
Now that they've seen it twice, we share the live link so they can click through and try it on their phone. By now we feel familiar, so the link gets clicked.
Live link
4
Day 12 — A gentle close
A short "should I take it down?" note. The light scarcity nudges anyone on the fence to reply.
Soft close
Reply at any point → the sequence stops automatically. No one who answers ever gets a follow-up meant for someone who didn't. A real person handles every reply from there.

How we protect our reputation

Built-in safeguards

✓
Verified addresses only
We only email addresses confirmed as real and deliverable — bad ones never get sent.
✓
Gradual ramp-up
Daily send limits that grow slowly, so inboxes trust us instead of flagging us.
✓
Stops on reply
The instant someone replies, follow-ups stop and a person takes over.
✓
Easy opt-out
Anyone can ask to stop and we remove them immediately — no exceptions.
✓
Screenshots, not attachments
The image shows inside the email itself — nothing to download, nothing suspicious.
✓
One master off-switch
We can pause every send instantly if anything looks off.
