Single-action prompt

One big "do this next" card + 2-3 secondary actions. Smartest for absolute beginner; risks feeling patronizing once you know the system.

Tradeoffs

Inbox matches "obvious next step" goal most directly — every item is something to act on. Scales as the system fills up.
Mission control is better for operators who want to know "how's the business doing" at a glance.
Single-action is the most beginner-friendly but feels limited once you have multiple things going on.

My recommendation: Inbox as the home. Mission-control numbers move to a "Status" page (which already exists). Single-action prompt is patronizing as a permanent home.
