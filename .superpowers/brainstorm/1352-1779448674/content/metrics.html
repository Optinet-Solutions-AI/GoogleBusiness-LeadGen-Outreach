Why these 6

The hero Needs You already covers acute action items, so the metrics don't need to repeat them. The 6 cards cover the 4 dimensions of a healthy pipeline:

  • Volume: Sites deployed · Active batches
  • Quality: Reply rate (with weekly delta)
  • Money in: Closed this month + MRR added
  • Money out: Spend this week against a cap
  • Opportunity: Pipeline value (replies × historical close $)

Each card is clickable and routes to a filtered view (e.g. clicking "Closed this month" → leads page filtered to closed_won, last 30 days).

Tell me in the terminal

Are these 6 right? Want to swap any? Common alternates: cost per lead, days to close, scraped count, qualified rate, average rating across deployed sites, warmup days remaining on the sending domain.
