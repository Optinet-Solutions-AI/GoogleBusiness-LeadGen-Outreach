How to test

CRM board

free
01

Board loads

  1. Sidebar → CRM.
Pass: nine columns (Not contacted → Lost) with counts; "Not contacted" shows 40 cards and a "+N more" link.
02

Drag a card

  1. Drag a card from Contacted to Replied.
  2. Open the lead (click card → "Open lead").
Pass: a toast "name → Replied"; the lead page shows stage Replied. Dropping into "Not contacted" is refused (column greys out).
Dragging only records the stage. No email or call is sent. Use a test lead.
03

Side panel: follow-up, owner, tags, note

  1. Click a card.
  2. Click "Tomorrow" under Next follow-up.
  3. Type an owner name, click away. Type tags hot, call back, click away.
  4. Add a note, click Add note.
Pass: "saved" toasts; the card now shows the date badge, tags hot and call-back, the owner; the note appears at the top of the timeline.
04

Timeline

  1. Open a lead that has had emails or an AI call.
Pass: emails sent/received, calls, stage changes and notes, newest first, each with a time.
05

Follow-up strip and filters

  1. Click "overdue", "today", "this week" in the strip on top.
  2. Search a name; filter by Owner and Tag.
  3. Click Clear.
Pass: the board narrows to matching cards each time; counts in the strip match what's set.
06

Stage from the panel

  1. In the side panel, change the Stage dropdown to Meeting booked.
Pass: the card jumps columns; a "Replied → Meeting booked" line appears in the timeline.

Reels

free
07

Make a Reel

  1. Open a client with at least 2 approved posts → Posts tab.
  2. Reels strip → Make a Reel. Leave the posts ticked, type an opening title, Render Reel.
  3. Wait. The strip polls by itself.
Pass: queued → rendering → ready within ~2 min; the video plays in the strip: opening card with logo, each post zooming slowly, crossfades, a thin progress bar on top.
If it shows "failed", send the red error text.
08

Download and delete

  1. Click the download icon on the ready Reel.
  2. Click the bin icon.
Pass: an MP4 named YYYY-MM_reel_N-posts.mp4 (1080×1920); after delete the card is gone.
09

Button is off with too few posts

  1. Pick a month with 0 or 1 approved posts.
Pass: "Make a Reel" is greyed out with a hint to approve 2 posts first.

OpenAI

cents
10

Contact finder on the Malta batch (already run)

  1. Open the Malta batch → BETA Centre, D Floorings, Brands International.
Pass: each has an email (info@betacentre.com.mt, info@dfloorings.com, hr@brands.com.mt) and a "Contact details found" box with the contact page link.

11

AI backgrounds

  1. Client → Posts → Make a post, then Generate with "AI background" on.
Pass: the post renders with a generated background; Spent shows about $0.05.

Ad Library

cents · after migration 056 + redeploy
12

Pull competitor ads

  1. Sidebar → Clients → Ad Library → Pull ads.
  2. Keyword flooring store, country Malta (or United States), Video only, Running now, Max 30.
  3. Check the estimate (≈ $0.17) → Pull ads. Wait 1–3 min; the page refreshes itself.
Pass: a grid of ads with a thumbnail, the advertiser's page name, "running" and days, a CTA; the Media filter shows only videos.
Try the URL mode too: paste a brand's Ad Library link (Ad Library → a page → copy the address bar).
13

Open, play, save

  1. Click an ad. The video plays in the drawer; text, CTA, landing page and "In Ad Library" links show.
  2. Click the star (Save). Add a tag and a note, click away.
  3. Set Show → Saved only.
Pass: "Saved — media copied" toast; the ad has a yellow star on its card; the expiry warning in the drawer disappears; the Saved filter lists it.
14

Make our version

  1. In a saved ad's drawer, pick client Deckor (or any client with a Brand Brain), tick Instagram + Facebook → Make our version.
  2. Read "Why it works", "Our brief" and the video script.
  3. Click "Open the draft post in Creative Studio" → Generate → review.
Pass: the analysis appears within ~15 s; the card shows "our version ×1"; the draft post opens in the client's Posts tab with the brief, and generates a post that uses only the client's own facts.
Nothing from the competitor's ad (footage, images, sentences, prices) should appear in our post. If it does, send it to me.
15

Delete

  1. Open an ad → bin icon.
Pass: the card is gone; the pull count in the Pull filter stays as it was.
