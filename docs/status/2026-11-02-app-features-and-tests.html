RateUp · 2 Nov 2026 · whole app

Almost everything works. Email, SMS and AI calls each need one thing switched on.

The list goes in sidebar order. Every item has a test below.

To switch on

  1. Email: copy ONGAGE_API_KEY and ONGAGE_TRANSACTIONAL_CAMPAIGN_ID from Vercel into .env, then redeploy Cloud Run. You
  2. AI calls: create the voizo-call-tick timer (test 24). You
  3. OpenAI: add credit. Needed for the contact finder and Creative Studio images. You
  4. SMS: Mobivate has to enable sending on our key. Mobivate
  5. Inventory email: pick the sender name. It's currently "Sam". Meny

Feature map

Overview

  • Live
    Today. What needs you now: replies, builds, sends.
  • Live
    Analytics. Funnel and campaign numbers.
  • Live
    Revenue. Totals from the setup fee and monthly price you record on each lead.
  • Live
    Status. Weekly, monthly and yearly counts.

Batches (finding leads)

  • Live
    Google Maps search. Pick a category, then a niche; country, optional city; Apify, Google Places or Outscraper. Shows the cost before running.
  • Live
    Closing businesses (free). WARN layoff notices and SEC bankruptcy filings.
  • Live
    Pause, Resume, Cancel. Cancel keeps the leads found so far.
  • Live
    Right country. Foreign results are blocked from outreach.
  • Live
    Website contact check (free). Footer, contact and about pages: email, phone, contact page link.
  • Needs credit
    OpenAI contact finder. Looks up the email and owner when the site has none. Up to 100 leads per batch, about 1–3¢ each.
  • Live
    Export. Download a batch as CSV.

Leads

  • Live
    Lead list. Search, filter, sort; import a CSV; bulk Build.
  • Live
    Build demo site. A personalized site, live on its own link, plus a screenshot.
  • Live
    Rebuild, Improve, Handover. Re-run a build; rebuild with the client's own photos and copy; attach their domain.
  • Live
    Meeting, notes, billing. Booked or done, notes, setup fee and monthly price.
  • Live
    Email checker. Checks the address is real before any send.
  • Live
    Right offer per lead. No site → build one. Weak site → improve it. Good site → AI services. Closing → buy inventory.

Clients

  • Live
    Make client. From a lead, or a new one.
  • Live
    Brand Brain. An AI profile from the website, socials and Google Maps reviews. Every fact sourced; new versions wait for your review.
  • Live
    Products and assets. Up to 10 featured products, logo, photos.
  • Live
    Creative Studio. Plan a month of posts, AI copy, three sizes, claim check, approve, download the pack.
  • Needs credit
    AI post backgrounds. OpenAI images. Without credit, posts still render without them.

Outreach

  • Blocked
    Email sequence. Day 0 text, day 4 screenshot, day 8 link, day 12 close. Waits on the Ongage keys.
  • Blocked
    Single emails. Cold email, inventory email, Inbox reply and compose. Same keys.
  • Live
    Mailboxes. lucas@, sophia@ and nathan@rateupdigital.com are connected and active.
  • Live
    Inbox. Pulls replies every few minutes. A reply, bounce or unsubscribe stops the sequence.
  • Live
    Campaigns. Email or AI-call campaigns: add leads, test send, launch, results.
  • 1 step
    AI calls (Voizo). Connected and checked. Needs its timer to start dialing.
  • Blocked
    SMS + intake form. One-time link to a form the client fills in. Waits on Mobivate.
  • Live
    Social DMs. List of social-only leads; log each DM you send by hand.
  • Live
    Contact-form check. Finds which leads have a contact form. Sends nothing.

Not built

AI calls

  1. Create the timer once (Git Bash):
gcloud scheduler jobs create http voizo-call-tick \
  --location=us-central1 --schedule="*/15 * * * *" \
  --uri="https://run.googleapis.com/v2/projects/pearl-view-491114/locations/us-central1/jobs/lead-batch-runner:run" \
  --http-method=POST --oauth-service-account-email=<same account as email-sequence-tick> \
  --message-body='{"overrides":{"containerOverrides":[{"env":[{"name":"MODE","value":"call"}]}]}}'
  1. Campaigns → AI call campaign → add a test lead with your phone → Activate.
  2. Wait up to 15 min, during the campaign's calling hours.
Pass: your phone rings; the result shows on the lead.
