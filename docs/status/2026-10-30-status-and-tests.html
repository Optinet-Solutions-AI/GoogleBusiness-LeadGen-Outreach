AI call

Each call uses Voizo minutes. First, switch on the call timer once. Paste this in Git Bash:

gcloud scheduler jobs create http voizo-call-tick --location=us-central1 --schedule="*/15 * * * *" --http-method=POST --uri="https://run.googleapis.com/v2/projects/pearl-view-491114/locations/us-central1/jobs/lead-batch-runner:run" --message-body='{"overrides":{"containerOverrides":[{"env":[{"name":"MODE","value":"call"}]}]}}' --oauth-service-account-email="vercel-trigger-sa@pearl-view-491114.iam.gserviceaccount.com"
  1. Put your own phone number on a test lead.
  2. Open Campaigns, create an AI call campaign, add that lead, click Activate.
  3. Wait up to 15 minutes, inside Voizo's calling hours (Malta time).
Expect: your phone rings, the AI agent talks, and the lead shows the call result.
Limit: 100 calls a day, one at a time. To stop all calling:
gcloud scheduler jobs pause voizo-call-tick --location=us-central1
