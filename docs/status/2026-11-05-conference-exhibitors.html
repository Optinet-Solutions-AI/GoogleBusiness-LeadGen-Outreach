How it works

  1. Batches → New batch → Category "Conference exhibitors". Choose the conference category (iGaming & betting is first), where (a country or All of Europe), the year, and optionally paste conference links. Blank links = the system finds the shows itself.
  2. Find the shows. OpenAI web search lists the conferences for that category and place (ICE Barcelona, SBC Summit Lisbon, SiGMA World Rome, …) with their exhibitor pages and floor plans. About 5–10¢ per batch.
  3. Read the exhibitors. Most shows use an interactive floor plan (ExpoFP) that publishes the exhibitor list openly: company, website, description, stand number. Free. Shows without one get their exhibitors page read by Gemini.
  4. Find the contacts. Website from the list, or found by name; then the free site crawl and, when there's still no email, the OpenAI contact finder (1–3¢ a lead): email, phone, contact page, owner / marketing contact.
  5. Pitch. Each lead is tagged Exhibitor with the show, date, city and stand. The lead page offers Email (booth offer): a short plain-text email that names the show and the stand. No website is ever built or pitched to these leads. AI-call campaigns work too.

Good to know

  • Works
    SiGMA World 2026. Its own website blocks automated visitors, but its floor plan (sigmaworld2026.expofp.com) is public and reads fully.
  • Skipped
    Sites with a bot check (Cloudflare "Just a moment…"). We don't go around those. The batch log names each skipped show; paste its ExpoFP floor-plan link in a second batch, or import the list as CSV.
  • Rule
    One company, one lead. A company at several shows is kept once (first show); the rest are logged as duplicates.
  • Rule
    Any 2026 show counts, past or upcoming. A stand this year is the signal, so there is no "too old" cut-off.
  • Decide
    Sender name. The booth email is signed "Sam", like the inventory email. Tell me the name to use.
  • Cost
    About $3–6 for a 200-exhibitor show (contact finding). The lists themselves are free. The estimate shows before you run.
