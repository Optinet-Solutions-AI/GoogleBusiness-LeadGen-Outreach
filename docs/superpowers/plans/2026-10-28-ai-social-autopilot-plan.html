AI Social Autopilot

How to add the AI Social Media Autopilot brief to the RateUp lead-gen app: what we can reuse, what is new, and the order to build it in.

Draft · not committed 28 Oct 2026 Brief: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-ai-social-autopilot-prd.md

Recommendation

  • Build it inside RateUp as the "AI services" product. It is the offer for leads that already have a good website, and a monthly upsell for hosting clients.
  • Start done-for-you, not self-serve. The RateUp operator runs each client's Brand Brain, creatives, approvals and publishing from the existing dashboard.
  • Make self-serve SaaS the last phase. The app has no customer login or tenant isolation today, and the brief assumes a five-developer team.
  • Apply for platform access now. Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn approvals take the longest and are outside our control.
  • Sell from Phase 2. Branded image posts with captions, approved by the client and posted by hand, are already a paid service before auto-publishing exists.

Why it fits RateUp

It fills the empty offer

The offer strategy says a lead with a healthy website must never get a website pitch. It should get AI services instead. That offer is currently undefined. Social Autopilot gives it a concrete product to sell.

It reuses work we already do

Every enriched lead already has its logo, brand colour, real site photos and Google reviews pulled. That is most of a first Brand Brain before the client has done anything.

It raises revenue per client

Hosting clients pay a setup fee and monthly hosting. A monthly content plan stacks on top and is recorded in the same per-deal billing card.

It gives outreach a new hook

The screenshot-first email works because the prospect sees their own demo. A "here are three posts we made for you" email can run the same way for has-website leads.

Website URL→Brand Brain→Creatives→Approval→Schedule→Publish→Measure

Data model

New tables, added as migrations from 045 onward. No existing table changes shape. A client workspace links back to the lead it came from.

TableHolds
client_workspacesOne per client. It links to the original lead and holds plan, status and the monthly AI spend cap.
brand_profilesThe Brand Brain as versioned structured data: voice, audiences, colours, CTAs and claims, each with its source page.
brand_productsProducts and services with confirmed descriptions, prices and images. Up to ten are suggested automatically.

social_connectionsThe OAuth link per client account, with encrypted tokens, scopes, expiry and supported formats.
creatives · creative_versionsA post idea and its fixed rendered versions per platform and aspect ratio.

scheduled_posts · publish_attemptsEach planned post, every attempt to publish it, and the platform's confirmed post id.

post_metricsReach, views and engagement pulled from each platform where the API allows it.

Phased roadmap

Sizes are relative: S is days, M is one to two weeks, L is several weeks for one developer. Phase 0 runs alongside everything else.

PHASE 0

Platform access and legal

Start now · in parallel

Approvals for publishing on behalf of clients take the longest and are outside our control.

Do

  • Register a Meta developer app, complete business verification and request review of the page and Instagram publishing permissions.
  • Apply for TikTok's Content Posting API and plan for its audit.
  • Get Google OAuth verification for the YouTube upload scope.
  • Request LinkedIn posting access.

Also needed

  • Public privacy policy and terms pages on rateupdigital.com.
  • A data-deletion endpoint, which Meta requires.
  • Test accounts on every network.

Done when

  • A test page and test Instagram account can post through the API in development mode.
  • Each review's status is tracked on the admin page.
PHASE 1

Brand Brain, operator-run

Size L

Build

  • "Create client" from any lead, which seeds the workspace from what enrichment already found.
  • A crawler that reads the about, products, services, FAQ, pricing and contact pages.
  • Gemini turns the pages into a structured profile with a source link for every fact.
  • An editable Brand Brain page, ten suggested products, and version history.

Reuses

  • The site logo, colour and photo extractors.
  • The website auditor and shared headless browser.
  • The Cloud Run job, with a new crawl mode.

Done when

  • Five real client sites produce a profile the operator accepts after light edits.
  • Every claim and price links to the page it came from.
PHASE 2

Creative Studio v1: static posts and approvals

Size L

Build

  • Caption and hashtag packs per platform.
  • Branded image posts and carousels from HTML templates, in square, portrait and vertical sizes.
  • AI-generated backgrounds and scenes that never alter the real product photo.
  • Approve, reject and request-changes on fixed versions. Any edit voids the approval.
  • Cost logged for every AI job.

Reuses

  • The demo-site template approach and the Chromium screenshot pipeline.
  • Gemini, plus OpenAI for images.
  • The new data table component for the media library.

Done when

  • The operator can make a month of approved posts for one client in under an hour.
  • The posts download as a pack for manual posting.
Sellable from here as a done-for-you monthly content plan, posted by hand.
PHASE 3

Calendar and Meta auto-publishing

Size L

Build

  • Recurring rules such as "Monday 10:00, Instagram and Facebook reel", with time zones, blackout dates and content rotation.
  • A calendar view. Drafts are created days ahead so there is time to approve.
  • A publish job that runs every 15 minutes and posts only approved versions to Facebook Pages and Instagram.
  • Duplicate protection, retries, token checks and failure alerts.

Reuses

  • The send-window time-zone logic.
  • The sequence scheduler's tick pattern and Cloud Scheduler.
  • Outreach-style event logging.

Done when

  • A real client page posts on schedule for two weeks with no missed or duplicate posts.
  • A post counts as published only when Meta confirms it.
Blocked by Phase 0. Meta approval must land first.
PHASE 4

Video and more networks

Size L+

Build

  • Remotion video templates such as product showcase, promotion and seasonal, with per-scene revision.
  • Optional AI video scenes, with a spending cap enforced per client.
  • TikTok posting in the form its audit requires, YouTube Shorts uploads, and LinkedIn posts.

Reuses

  • The Chromium-ready Cloud Run image.
  • The publish interface from Phase 3.

Done when

  • A 15–30 second branded product video renders with an exact logo, price and subtitles.
  • It publishes to every network whose approval has landed.
PHASE 5

Analytics and the AI marketing agent

Size M

Build

  • Pull reach and engagement per post where each API allows it.
  • Generate content ideas automatically from the Brand Brain, avoiding recently used products and angles.
  • Check every price, discount and testimonial against approved Brand Brain facts before rendering.
  • Reference-inspired posts that analyse an example and save only the analysis, never the example itself.

Reuses

  • The analytics and KPI pages.
  • The Gemini prompt pipelines.

Done when

  • A monthly report per client shows posts, reach and the best-performing themes.
  • Next month's ideas take that report into account.
PHASE 6

Self-serve SaaS

Size L+ · decide later

Build

  • Customer sign-up and login, with owner, admin, editor, approver and viewer roles.
  • Strict per-workspace data isolation in the database and storage.
  • Stripe subscriptions, AI credits and spending caps.
  • A platform admin console.

Open question

  • It could stay in this app, or split into its own product sharing the same database, as voice calling did.
  • Splitting keeps the operator tool simple.

Done when

  • A new business can sign up, connect accounts, approve posts and pay without operator help.

Risks

Platform approvals

Review timelines are outside our control. TikTok public posting stays restricted until its audit passes. Phases must not promise a network before its approval lands.

Copying competitors

Reference-inspired content must produce original work. Store the analysis, not the competitor's media. Editing someone else's file needs a recorded rights confirmation.

Wrong claims

AI must not invent prices, discounts or testimonials. Anything factual has to exist in the approved Brand Brain with a source.

AI video cost

Video generation is the expensive part. Per-job cost logging and a monthly cap per client must exist before Phase 4 ships.

Team capacity

The brief assumes five developers. The done-for-you order is designed so each phase earns money before the next starts.

Privacy and GDPR

Client tokens and media are sensitive. EU clients need the GDPR review that CLAUDE.md already flags as open.

Decisions needed

  1. Start done-for-you, as recommended, or go straight to self-serve SaaS? This changes Phases 1–3 the most.
  2. Pricing. Is it a monthly add-on for hosting clients, a standalone plan for has-website leads, or both?
  3. Pilot clients. Pick three to five real businesses to build Phase 1 and 2 against.
  4. Image provider. OpenAI images, Gemini images, or both behind the adapter. Choose after a quality and cost test on pilot brands.
  5. Developer pack. The brief mentions seven more files: screens, API guides, schema and backlog. Share them if they exist, and this plan will be reconciled with them.
