How we work

A clear plan — not just an adjustment.

Step 01

Assess

We start with a full movement screening and a proper diagnosis. Before we touch anything, we find out where the problem actually starts — which is rarely where it hurts.

Step 02

Treat

Adjustments, soft tissue work, and targeted rehab exercises, combined to fit the injury. You leave each session with something to work on, not just a feeling that wears off by morning.

Step 03

Prevent

We build you a movement program so it doesn't come back. The goal is to discharge you stronger than you walked in — and keep you out of the clinic.
